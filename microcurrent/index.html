The quick guide · updated November 2026

Does at-home microcurrent work?

At-home microcurrent gives a temporary lifted, toned look when used consistently; it uses a low electrical current with a conductive gel. Results fade if you stop, so it suits people who'll stick with a few minutes most days.

What to look for

Adjustable intensity, clear guidance on how long to use it, a conductive gel that's easy to buy again, and comfortable prongs or spheres for jawline and cheeks.

Who should not use microcurrent

Skip it if you have a pacemaker or other implanted electrical device, are pregnant, have epilepsy, or have active skin infections; ask a doctor if unsure.

FAQ

How long until you see microcurrent results?

Many people notice a temporary lift right after a session; ongoing results usually need weeks of regular use.

Do you need conductive gel?

Yes. The gel lets the current pass properly and protects the skin; using a device on dry skin can feel uncomfortable.

Microcurrent or LED mask?

Microcurrent targets a lifted, toned look; LED masks target glow, fine lines or blemishes. They solve different things.
