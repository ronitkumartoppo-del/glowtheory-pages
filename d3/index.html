FAQ your questions, answered

Is the self care planner printable really free?

Yes. The self care planner printable will be free to download. It's launching soon, and this page will be updated with the download as soon as it's ready.

Can I print it at home?

Yes. It's designed to print on a regular home printer on standard paper. Print one page each week, or print a few at once so your next week is ready to go.

How often should I fill it in?

Fill in your intentions and checklist once a week, ideally during a Sunday reset. Then take a minute each day to tick off what you did and note your mood.

What should I put on my self-care checklist?

Keep it small and realistic: drink more water, a short walk, skincare, an earlier night, time outside, a screen-free hour, calling a friend or booking that appointment you keep putting off. Three to five items a week is plenty.
