Mindful Eating Tips for Beginnersslow down and actually enjoy your food

These mindful eating tips for beginners aren't a diet and there are no rules to follow. They're just small ways to slow down, notice your food, and enjoy meals instead of eating on autopilot while scrolling (guilty). Try one or two, and if you'd like more, there's a short ebook below.

5 gentle mindful eating tips

No counting, no "good" or "bad" foods, just a little more attention.

  1. Pause for one breath before you eat

    Before the first bite, take one slow breath and look at your plate. It's a tiny reset that helps you arrive at the meal.

  2. Put the phone in another room

    Try one meal a day without a screen. You may be surprised how much more you taste and notice.

  3. Check in with your hunger

    Halfway through, ask yourself: how hungry am I now, on a scale of one to ten? There's no right answer. It's just about noticing.

  4. Eat with all your senses

    Notice the colors, smells, textures and sounds of your food. Putting your fork down between bites makes this easier.

  5. Get curious about cravings

    When you want a snack, ask gently: am I hungry, or tired, bored or stressed? Whatever the answer, you can respond with kindness, not guilt.
