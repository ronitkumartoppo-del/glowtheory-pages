A simple self-care checklist: daily, weekly and monthly ideas

Daily basics

Daily self-care is mostly maintenance: drink water, move your body, eat a real meal, get some daylight, take a few minutes for something you enjoy, and go to bed at a reasonable time. Ticking these off keeps the foundation steady.

Weekly rituals

Once a week: a reset of your space, a meal plan, a self-care night (bath, face mask, a film you love), a check-in with a friend and a review of the week ahead. Weekly rituals stop small things from piling up.

Monthly check-ins

Once a month: review your goals, declutter one area, check your budget and subscriptions, and book any appointment you have been putting off. Monthly check-ins catch what daily and weekly routines miss.

Start with two

Do not start everything at once. Choose two daily items and one weekly ritual for the first month. Add more only when those feel automatic.

Make it visible

Print the checklist and put it where you will see it: the fridge, your desk or the bathroom mirror. Visibility is half the habit.

When to level up

If the checklist helps, a weekly planner or a Sunday reset routine can build on it. If it does not, simplify further. Self-care should make life lighter, not heavier.
